# Art Direction

Create a Direction, not a list of fashionable ingredients.

## Derive from the product

Before visual choices, explore:

- objects and instruments;
- materials and light;
- environments and spatial relationships;
- vocabulary and metaphors;
- natural colors;
- rhythms and behavior;
- the human task and emotional pressure.

Use this world to generate possibilities. Do not force literal metaphors.

## Declare one coherent thesis

A Direction includes:

- product interpretation;
- compositional model;
- hierarchy and density;
- typography strategy;
- color strategy;
- surface/depth strategy;
- motion thesis;
- identifying signature;
- system potential;
- risks.

Every choice should reinforce the thesis. Difference without coherence is noise.

## Anti-default ledger

Name likely category defaults before creating. Do not ban them universally. State:

- why they are likely here;
- when they remain valid;
- alternative mechanisms.

A common choice is acceptable when it is deliberate and well executed.

## Divergence

When assigned a lens, commit to it. Do not inspect peer candidates. A candidate must differ on at least three material axes from the expected default or other assigned lenses.

Do not create five color variants of the same layout.

## Signature

Define one identifying structural, visual, or interaction element that belongs to this product. Explain how it repeats without becoming a gimmick.

## Visual specimens

Render only enough to judge:

- first-view hierarchy;
- the signature;
- a central component;
- at least one non-happy state;
- typography and surface relationships;
- responsive viability when material.

Do not hide weak structure behind abstract mood art.

## System potential

Explain how the Direction scales to other Surfaces, content lengths, states, and viewports. A strong hero with no component language is not yet a system.

## Output

Return a Direction matching `framework/schemas/direction.schema.json`, Evidence plan, and risks. Do not implement production code or approve yourself.
