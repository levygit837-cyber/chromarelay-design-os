# Art Direction phases

The art-director role appears in four workflows. Your Phase Packet's `phase` field names which one you are in; produce that phase's output, not a full Direction, unless the phase asks for one.

## CREATE

| Phase | Produce | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| `domain-exploration` | Domain Map, Anti-default Ledger, Creative Opportunities | At least three materially different creative territories are available |
| `direction-divergence` | One Direction candidate under the assigned lens | The candidate diverges materially on at least three axes |
| `visual-specimens` | Visual specimens and risk notes | Eligible candidates carry comparable evidence |

`visual-specimens` passes the `direction-eligibility` gate, which removes Directions failing product truth, constraints, state support, responsiveness, or system potential. Every specimen exists to let that gate be decided from the artifact.

## EXPLORE

| Phase | Produce | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| `define-axes` | Exploration Axes and lens assignments | Lenses are materially different and product-relevant |
| `independent-concepts` | One Direction candidate under the assigned lens | Candidates differ materially on at least three axes |
| `prototype` | Visual specimens and system-potential notes | Evidence is comparable across candidates |

`define-axes` is the one phase where you name the axes rather than receive a lens. Choose axes that force structurally different answers; two axes that resolve to the same layout are one axis.

## REDESIGN

`target-world-divergence` produces Direction candidates from the Redesign Charter, Product Truths, and an anti-reference summary, and passes `direction-eligibility`. Done when eligible replacement worlds exist.

The existing design is product evidence and anti-reference. Read it for what the product actually does and for which defaults are already spent; do not read it as visual authority. When a current visual choice reappears in your Direction, the ledger has to show why it survived on its merits.

## REFINE

`focused-options` produces focused options plus a comparison from the Refinement Hypothesis, and passes the `direction-tournament` gate. Done when one mechanism is selected.

Options here vary one mechanism against the standing Baseline, so the comparison isolates that mechanism. State what each option changes and what it holds constant.
