# Pairwise comparison

Reached from [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) when the packet holds more than one specimen: a Direction tournament, or a candidate against a frozen Baseline.

## Procedure

1. **Randomize** the order you view them in, and record the order you used. If the packet arrived pre-randomized, keep that order and say so.
2. **Score each one alone**, to the full rubric, before you place them side by side. A score formed during comparison is a relative score wearing an absolute number.
3. **Pick a winner per material criterion**, one criterion at a time. Name the criterion, the winner, and what decided it.
4. **Name the overall winner**, with confidence and the trade-off you accepted to get there.
5. **Say what the loser did better.** Every one of those is a candidate to carry into the winner, and it is the only place that information survives.

Evaluate in this order: visual strength first, then system potential, states, scalability, and implementation risk. Leading with implementation risk selects the safest specimen rather than the strongest.

## Hybrids

Recommend a hybrid only when a specific criterion has a specific winner and the merge is one identified move. Describe it as that move. "Combine the best of both" resolves to nothing an art-director can execute, and averaging two coherent specimens reliably produces an incoherent third.

## Ties

Report a tie as a tie, with what would break it — a state, a viewport, real content, a second critic. A material tie is escalated to a human decision, and an invented winner replaces that decision with noise.

## Baseline comparisons

The Baseline is frozen and shipped: it carries evidence of working that the candidate does not have. Two things follow.

- Check that the candidate preserves the tasks the Baseline supports. A candidate that wins on quality and drops a task is a `structural return`, not a win.
- The candidate has to beat the Baseline, not tie it. Report an even outcome as "no improvement demonstrated" and let the trade-off be accepted explicitly or the change rolled back.
