# Foundations and primary references

This repository was designed from primary sources and first-party documentation.

## Engineering discipline

Matt Pocock's engineering skills informed the architecture:

- [codebase-design](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/engineering/codebase-design): deep modules, small interfaces, seams, adapters, leverage and locality;
- [Design It Twice](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/engineering/codebase-design/DESIGN-IT-TWICE.md): independent alternative interfaces before commitment;
- [domain-modeling](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/engineering/domain-modeling): canonical vocabulary and sparse ADRs;
- [TDD](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/engineering/tdd): tests at pre-agreed seams and vertical tracer bullets;
- [to-spec](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/engineering/to-spec/SKILL.md) and [to-tickets](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/engineering/to-tickets/SKILL.md): synthesis and implementable vertical slices;
- [wayfinder](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/engineering/wayfinder/SKILL.md): decision maps for work that is still under fog of war;
- [research](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/engineering/research/SKILL.md): primary-source research captured in-repo.
