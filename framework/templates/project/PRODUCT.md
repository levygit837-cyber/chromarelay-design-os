# Product

## User

Who the actual user is, where they are, what happened immediately before use, and what happens after.

## Core task

The verb that defines the primary work.

## Desired outcome

What becomes true after successful use.

## Product truths

Facts, content, behavior, data, and constraints that creative work must not invent or silently change.

## Desired perception

Specific qualities the product should communicate. Avoid empty language such as only “clean” or “modern”.

## Differentiation

What makes this product unlike generic products in the same category.

## Creative space

Decisions deliberately left open for exploration.

## Open questions

Material uncertainties with owner and confidence.
