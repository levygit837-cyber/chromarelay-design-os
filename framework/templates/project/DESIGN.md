# Design System

## Thesis

The selected creative Direction in one precise paragraph.

## Product relationship

How visual decisions emerge from the product domain and task.

## Composition grammar

Rules and ranges for hierarchy, focal points, rhythm, density, proportion, navigation, and responsive transformation.

## Typography

Roles, relationships, hierarchy mechanics, language coverage, numeral behavior, loading, and exceptions. Exact values live in canonical tokens.

## Color and surfaces

Semantic roles, temperature, contrast, elevation, depth strategy, and use of accent.

## Shape and spacing

Radius logic, primitive spacing, semantic spacing aliases, control sizing, page gutters, and content measure.

## Components

Component principles and pointers to canonical contracts or Storybook.

## Motion

Purpose, duration ranges, easing roles, reduced-motion behavior, and prohibited accidental motion.

## Signature

The identifying element or behavior and conditions that keep it from becoming a gimmick.

## Surface variants

How PERSUADE, OPERATE, READ, and EXPERIENCE Surfaces adapt the same system.

## Do and avoid

Contextual guidance with justification, not universal aesthetic bans.

## Exceptions

Pointers to scoped exceptions.
