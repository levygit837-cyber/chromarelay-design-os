# Direction — Reading Room (direction-reading-room)

## Thesis
The agentic session is an essay argued in the center and proven in the margin: one calm idea per viewport, with every tool claim numbered where it is made and verifiable where it is checked.

## Product interpretation
This product is a long editorial document that happens to execute commands: a builder reads an argument in the center and checks its citations without leaving the page. The agentic chat surface is therefore a reading room with a workbench behind a glass wall — the essay on the desk, the machinery visible but silent until cited. Calm-reading vs capability tension is explicit and structural: calm wins the default viewport because sustained verification is the real task, and capability wins the first gesture because every claim is numbered, every number opens its note, and every note summons its sheet. Restraint here is not minimalism; it is accountability made legible — one idea per viewport so that trust can keep up with speed.

## Domain world
- The library behind glass: bench, library, and mission wall overlap, with the user over the agent's shoulder. The center is a reading desk; the wings withdraw to slim rails and evidence arrives as summoned reading sheets.
- Paper printouts marked up by hand and the marginalia tradition: the field already annotates long outputs in the margin. Translated into a citation layout binding prose, note, and sheet.
- Burst-pause-verify rhythm: deliberate typing, fast mixed-duration tool bursts, streaming prose, then verification or interruption. Motion becomes breath rather than theater.
- Trust-but-verify anxiety plus vigilance fatigue: accountable delegation to a fast junior. Every claiming turn carries numerals or an explicit empty-trace line; calm is load-bearing.
- Bench materials and light: screen glow, syntax stained glass, status lamps, separation by rules and wells. Warm paper with hairline rules; light means status only, pip plus label.

## Composition
Editorial page with marginalia. Measure-constrained article column (60-72ch) holds one idea per viewport; tool activity lives as numbered marginal notes in the gutter (footnote rows expanding in place at narrow widths). Sidebars withdraw to slim rails opening on demand; verification views are full-bleed reading sheets on a scrim when summoned, each citing its numeral range. Follow-ups are a calm ordered list. Density is lowest by design; all density is spent inside expanded notes and summoned sheets.

## Typography
Editorial serif or serif-adjacent reading face at true text size with generous leading; hierarchy from size and rhythm, not badges. Small structured sans for chrome and rail labels; mono quarantined to code and evidence detail. Superscript numerals at sidenote grade keep the citation thread visible without breaking the sentence.

## Color
Quiet grounds carry reading; saturated color is rationed to meaning and redundant with label plus shape. Warm paper ground, ink text at AA, desaturated warm-gray chrome, and exactly three signal jobs: status triad with label plus pip, diff semantics with +/- context, reference blue for links and browser. No ambient gradient, no status-by-color-alone; the direction survives a grayscale test.

## Surfaces
Warm paper plus glass. One continuous warm matte ground with hairline column rules; marginal notes as ruled sidenotes, code and diffs in quiet inset wells. Exactly two depth levels: the ambient page and the summoned sheet on a scrim. Only send and retry earn decisive elevation.

## Motion
Breath: fades, a single height ease for disclosure, a quiet caret for streaming, rails moving as one gesture. Running advances a text label plus pip with no pulse; interrupt and retry acknowledge without flash. Reduced-motion resolves instantly to the final labeled state; every state is legible statically.

## Signature
Marginal evidence numerals. Each consequential tool claim carries a superscript numeral matched to a ruled marginal note and to the summoned sheet header. Recurrence: inline claim, margin note, and sheet header on every claiming turn, every session, all three views. Regulation: only consequential calls earn numerals, inline runs cap before collapsing to a labeled range (e.g. notes 3-7), user prose and steering never carry numerals, empty-trace turns show an explicit unnumbered line, and sheet headers cite the exact summoning range.

## Anti-defaults
Examined and replaced: dark console theme; card piles; chip-cloud follow-ups with badge pills; shimmer, pulse, and toast feedback; persistent three-pane dashboard with gradient identity. Each default was kept only where it earns its place (dark inside code wells, elevation on send and retry) and otherwise replaced by paper, rules, lists, breath, and citation.

## System potential
A rule system, not a layout. Long sessions gain a quiet marginal index; short answers still compose on the fixed measure. Streaming, running, failure-in-place with retry, empty, and overflow states are all set editorially. 1440px shows column plus gutters and rails; mid widths dock one rail and overlay the other; 375px is a single column with drawer rails and footnote rows, composer always reachable, no horizontal scroll. All three verification views share one sheet template; future surfaces inherit essay center, cited margin, summoned sheets.

## Risks
Underplays capability at wide widths; failures could feel too quiet; wide viewports risk emptiness; numeral fatigue in heavy turns; gutter-to-footnote collapse doubles the disclosure pattern and must stay keyboard-clean.

## Divergence axes
Compositional model, information density, motion role, surface logic, and typographic voice — each structurally distinct from the category default and from dossier, ops-board, and spine-plus-ticket lenses.

## Evidence plan
First view at 1440px; signature in place with sheet header; marginal-note expansion component; failure-in-place plus empty and streaming states; typography and surface at density; responsive 375px single-column behavior.
