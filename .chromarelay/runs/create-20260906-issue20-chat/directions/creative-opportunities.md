## Territory 1 — The Flight Recorder

- **Seed image:** an aircraft flight recorder and a lab notebook laid side by side: nothing decorative, everything timestamped, every claim initialed by its evidence.
- **Compositional model:** dossier. A calm central reading column (Tier 1) flanked by a ruled audit rail: each assistant turn carries its tool rows docked beneath it, and the right wing is an evidence bench that cites back to the turn (`Session 12 · Turn 4 → files`). The page reads top-to-bottom as a record, not as a chat.
- **Density:** low in the center, high in the wings. Prose breathes; the trace and sidebars go dense and tabular without apology.
- **Motion role:** motion as attestation. Almost nothing moves except state transitions that certify work: `running → succeeded / failed` flips a status pip with a label change, streaming resolves skeleton into final rows. No ambient animation.
- **Surface logic:** paper ledger. Conversation on a continuous matte ground, trace rows separated by hairline rules rather than elevation, verification views as stamped annex pages with their own headers. Depth means evidentiary depth, not shadows.
- **Typographic voice:** affidavit. A highly legible reading serif or quiet grotesk for prose, small-caps rail labels (`YOU / AGENT / TOOL`), and tabular mono reserved strictly for evidence (paths, commands, durations, hashes). Voice contrast does the hierarchy work.
- **Signature hint (not a decision):** the *cite-back rule* — every verification view carries the turn-and-tool reference that produced it, so evidence never detaches from its claim.
- **What it would prove:** that trust comes from structure, and that a dense workbench can still read calmly when tiers are disciplined.
- **Risk if it wins:** could read archival or slow; streaming theater must be resisted; failed states must stay urgent on a quiet ground.

## Territory 2 — The Control Room

- **Seed image:** mission control at night: a wall of status boards, every system nominal or not at a glance, the operator's hand always near the abort switch.
- **Compositional model:** operations board. The center column is narrower and flanked by persistent high-signal rails; the tool trace renders as a status board (kind / target / status / duration columns) rather than as prose appendages. The right wing defaults to the most anomalous view, not to a fixed tab.
- **Density:** high everywhere, deliberately. Session list shows state badges, trace rows show durations and exit codes inline, verification views show compact tables. Density is the capability signal (brief §7: capable like a control room).
- **Motion role:** motion as signal. Running tools pulse in place with a reduced-motion-safe opacity step, failures flash once then settle to a persistent labeled badge, view switches cut rather than slide. Motion answers "what needs my eyes right now."
- **Surface logic:** dark instrument bench. Deep matte ground, inset wells for live regions (streaming, running tools), raised caps only for the composer and for interrupt/retry — the two controls that change the world. Color is reserved for status and always paired with text + icon (C-A2).
- **Typographic voice:** console tabular. Mono-forward throughout the trace and sidebars,一则 proportional text only for assistant prose summaries. Numbers align, statuses share one column, scanning is vertical.
- **Signature hint (not a decision):** the *anomaly-first wing* — the right sidebar promotes whichever view holds the newest failure or running tool, with a labeled reason ("showing GitHub: this turn pushed").
- **What it would prove:** that an agentic surface can feel powerful without feeling cluttered, provided every pixel answers a status question.
- **Risk if it wins:** could overwhelm reading (Tier 1 drowned by Tier 2), could fail AA contrast on dark status colors, could fatigue through perpetual motion if discipline slips.

## Territory 3 — The Reading Room

- **Seed image:** a quiet editorial reading room with a workbench behind a glass wall: the essay on the desk, the machinery visible but silent.
- **Compositional model:** editorial page with marginalia. The conversation is a generous measure-constrained article column; tool activity lives as numbered marginal notes in the gutter (or as footnote-style rows that expand in place), and the sidebars withdraw to slim rails that open on demand. Verification views are full-bleed reading sheets when summoned.
- **Density:** lowest of the four. One idea per viewport, generous leading, follow-ups set as a calm list rather than a chip cloud. Density is spent entirely inside expanded tool detail and inside the summoned verification sheet.
- **Motion role:** motion as breath. Page transitions fade, disclosure opens with a single height ease (with a reduced-motion instant fallback), streaming is a quiet caret with no shimmer. The room never startles the reader.
- **Surface logic:** warm paper and glass. One continuous warm ground, no cards, hairline column rules, verification sheets float above on a scrim only when called. Calm is load-bearing: it protects sustained reading across long sessions.
- **Typographic voice:** editorial. Serif or serif-adjacent reading face for assistant prose at a true text size, small structured sans for chrome, mono only inside code/detail. Hierarchy is carried by size and rhythm, not by badges.
- **Signature hint (not a decision):** *marginal evidence numerals* — each tool claim carries a numeral that matches a marginal note and, when open, the corresponding verification view. Citation as layout.
- **What it would prove:** that the primary task (direct and verify) is still a reading task, and that restraint scales to long sessions better than density theater.
- **Risk if it wins:** could underplay capability (reads as article, not workbench); tool failures could feel too quiet; 1440px could feel empty unless the withdrawn rails are genuinely one keystroke/click away.

## Territory 4 — The Work-Order Rail

- **Seed image:** a workshop job board with a vertical time spine: each job is a ticket clipped to the rail, each ticket moves from queued to done, the wall tells the day's story at a glance.
- **Compositional model:** spine and tickets. A persistent vertical time/progress spine runs alongside the center column; each assistant turn is a work order bundling its prose plus its tool tickets (Write / Read / Edit / Bash / WebSearch as ticket rows with kind, target, status). The right wing mirrors the selected ticket. Time is spatial.
- **Density:** medium, rhythmic. Turns alternate between compact ticket rows and one expanded detail well; the spine compresses history so long sessions stay navigable (jump to turn, jump to failure).
- **Motion role:** motion as progress along the rail. Tickets slide through `queued → running → succeeded / failed` positions on the spine, the composer echoes optimistically then docks to its ticket, long Bash/test runs show determinate-feeling staged progress built from stub durations. Motion narrates the loop.
- **Surface logic:** ticket stack. Turns are clipped cards on the rail (the one territory where cards are structural, not decorative), the spine is a continuous rule with node markers, verification views are the ticket's attachments. Elevation is reserved for the active ticket only.
- **Typographic voice:** workmanlike grotesk. Sturdy sans for tickets and spine labels, mono for targets and commands, larger numerals for turn order. The voice says job, not journal.
- **Signature hint (not a decision):** the *spine navigator* — the time rail doubles as session navigation (scrub to any turn, failures marked as red nodes with counts), solving long-session orientation without a separate minimap.
- **What it would prove:** that agentic time — bursts, waits, retries — can be made legible as space, and that resumption starts with "where was I" rather than with scrolling.
- **Risk if it wins:** the spine could become chrome that competes with content; ticket cards could regress into card soup (D7) unless elevation rules are absolute; narrow viewports must reconcile two vertical rails (spine + drawers) without h-scroll.

## How the territories differ (axis matrix)

| Axis | T1 Flight Recorder | T2 Control Room | T3 Reading Room | T4 Work-Order Rail |
|------|--------------------|-----------------|-----------------|--------------------|
| Compositional model | Dossier: reading column + ruled audit rail | Ops board: status grid with anomalous wing | Editorial page + marginalia, withdrawable rails | Spine + tickets; time as space |
| Density | Low center / high wings | High everywhere | Lowest; density on demand | Medium, rhythmic compression |
| Motion role | Attestation (state flips) | Signal (what needs eyes) | Breath (quiet continuity) | Progress along rail |
| Surface logic | Paper ledger, rules not shadows | Dark instrument wells + raised action caps | Warm paper + glass sheets | Ticket stack, active-only elevation |
| Typographic voice | Affidavit (serif/grotesk + rail caps + evidence mono) | Console tabular mono-forward | Editorial serif reading face | Workmanlike grotesk + turn numerals |

No two territories share more than one axis value. Palette-only variation was deliberately excluded: each territory would survive a grayscale test because its difference is structural.

## Handoff to direction-divergence

- Any single territory above can seed one Direction candidate; T1+T3 and T2+T4 are the most contrasting pairs for a two- or four-candidate slate.
- Each candidate will still need: thesis sentence, full field set, signature with recurrence rules, system-potential proof (all states, all widths, all three sidebar views), and specimens at real density per the skill's evidence plan.
- Open structural questions for candidates (not decisions): exact 375px drawer/tab mechanics; whether the trace defaults expanded or collapsed per turn; exact mode taxonomy in the steering console; spine-vs-ledger behavior in sessions with 50+ turns.
