# Direction — Flight Recorder (T1)

## Thesis
Every agent claim is entered into a calm, ruled dossier where prose reads and evidence certifies.

## Product interpretation
This product is an accountability record for delegated coding work, not a chatbot: a session-scoped dossier where each instruction, each tool act, and each piece of evidence is entered, ordered, and citable, so a builder can direct fast, verify without leaving the surface, and resume tomorrow knowing exactly what happened.

## Domain world (crossings, translated into mechanism)
1. Wells and rules, not card piles: the bench separates live regions as inset wells and completed work as ruled rows, with shadows shallow and rare — crossed into a paper-ledger surface where rules do the grouping and only send, interrupt, and retry earn elevation.
2. Flight-recorder and lab-notebook accountability: every consequential step leaves a timestamped, retrievable entry initialed by its evidence — crossed one level further from depiction into mechanism as docked tool rows plus the cite-back rule, so no assistant claim stands on prose alone.
3. Burst-pause-verify rhythm: slow deliberate typing, fast mixed-duration tool bursts (millisecond Reads, seconds-long Bash/tests), then verification, steering, or interruption — crossed into motion as attestation, where skeletons resolve into rows and status flips certify completion with no ambient animation.
4. Bench, library, and mission-wall spatial order: the user directs from a central reading desk, glances left to resume and right to verify — crossed into session-scoped wings where the left wing resumes (projects group sessions) and the right wing proves (evidence scoped to the active Session.id).
5. Trust-but-verify anxiety under vigilance fatigue: delegating consequential work to a fast junior while staying accountable is exhausting — crossed into split density (calm low-density center under a dense but disciplined workbench) plus in-place failure with retry, so vigilance costs less.

## Composition
Dossier: a calm central reading column (Tier 1 conversation plus composer) flanked by ruled audit wings. Tier 1 is the current answer with its docked tool rows; Tier 2 is session resumption plus tool summaries; Tier 3 is tool detail plus the right-wing evidence bench (browser, files, GitHub), each annex page with its own header. Tool rows dock directly beneath the assistant turn that claims them — never in a detached timeline — so the page accretes as a record. Density is split: low in the center (60-70ch measure, airy leading) and high in the wings (dense tabular kind/target/status/duration rows). Responsive: 1440px three docked panes; mid widths one wing overlays; 375px single column with wings as drawers and the composer persistently reachable; no horizontal page scroll.

## Typography — affidavit register
Three voices with strict jurisdictions: a quiet grotesk or transitional serif for prose at true text size carries the reading; small-caps YOU / AGENT / TOOL rail labels at 11px with tracking carry role and order; tabular mono at 12-13px carries evidence only (paths, commands, durations, refs). Hierarchy comes from voice contrast and rule position, not display size; the loudest element is a status label. Chrome uses a small structured sans subordinate to prose. Mono outside evidence is a defect.

## Color — quiet grounds, rationed signal
Ground means calm paper continuity; chrome means desaturated steel subordinate to the center. Signal means the status triad (success green, running amber, failed red) rationed to pips, badges, and diff tokens, always label plus icon for AA and grayscale survival. Reference blue means linkage only. Diff green/red mean added/removed only, confined to evidence. Rationing rule: no saturated fill larger than a pip, badge, or diff token without an adjacent label; no ambient gradients or glow.

## Surfaces — paper ledger, rules not shadows
One continuous matte reading ground; hairline rules separate turns and docked rows. Live regions read as shallow inset wells; completed work as ruled rows; verification views as stamped annex pages with header rule plus cite-back line. Only the composer and world-changing controls (send, interrupt, retry) are raised. Empty, loading, and error states replace content in place in the same ruled slot — never overlays or toasts.

## Motion — attestation
Nothing moves except transitions that certify work. Running flips to succeeded/failed as a pip-plus-label swap; skeleton rows resolve into final rows staged from stub durations; disclosure expands in place with one short ease; view switches cut. Streaming prose shows only a quiet caret. Interrupt and retry acknowledge instantly (optimistic echo under ~300ms). Reduced motion collapses everything to instant swaps that stay legible statically.

## Signature — the cite-back rule
Every verification view and expanded tool detail carries its turn-and-tool reference (Session N / Turn M plus kind and target). It recurs in the annex header, the expanded tool-row footer, error and empty annex headers, and the mobile sheet header. Regulation: exactly one cite-back line per evidence container, in 12px tabular mono directly beneath the view title; never inside prose, never repeated per inner row; session-level emptiness reads Session N — no turns yet. Recurrence reads as certification, not tic.

## Anti-defaults (examined, not banned)
1. Bubbly chat bubbles: default from commodity chat; right only for social chat; replaced by small-caps rail labels on a continuous ground.
2. Card piles with shadows: default fastest grouping; right only for heterogeneous dashboards; replaced by ruled ledger with elevation reserved for world-changing controls.
3. Ambient AI gradient identity: default modernity signal; right only for marketing; replaced by rationed signal color that always certifies something.
4. Streaming theater plus auto-dismissing toasts: default liveliness; right only for transient confirmations; replaced by in-place attestation and in-place failure with retry.
5. Literal dark terminal-green theme: default technical shorthand; right only for full-time dark ops; replaced by a paper ledger with a label-plus-pip status system that works light and survives grayscale.
6. Single lonely chat column with hidden verification: default simplest build; right only for one-shot Q&A; replaced by session-scoped wings with a citing-back evidence bench.

## System potential
Ruled rows, status pips with labels, mono evidence slots, annex headers with cite-back lines, and the raised composer cap compose every surface: session history, diffs, browser excerpts, and GitHub refs each reuse them. Long sessions collapse settled rows to summaries while turn numerals and cite-backs stay visible, plus a turn/failure jump index. Short and empty content renders as ruled placeholders with explicit labels and next actions. Loading resolves skeletons in place; errors retry in place; overflow truncates mono with in-place expand. Scales 1440px docked through mid-width overlay to 375px drawers with the composer always reachable and the cite-back slot intact.

## Risks
Archival or slow reading; failures losing urgency on quiet ground; wing density leaking into the reading path; ledger monotony over 50-plus turns; mono drift collapsing the affidavit voice; long stub runs feeling frozen without shimmer unless skeleton staging is clearly progressive.

## Divergence axes
Compositional model (dossier versus chat column, ops grid, marginalia, spine-tickets); density distribution (low-center/high-wings versus uniform, high-everywhere, lowest-everywhere, rhythmic); motion role (attestation versus decoration, signal, breath, progress); surface logic (rules versus cards, wells, glass, ticket stacks); typographic voice (affidavit tri-voice versus console-mono, editorial-serif, workmanlike grotesk).
