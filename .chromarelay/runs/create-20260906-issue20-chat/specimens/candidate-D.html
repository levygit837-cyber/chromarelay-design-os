Voice 1 · reading prose (Georgia serif)

The refresh race is a classic single-flight problem: two tabs notice expiry together and both spend the one good refresh token. The second spend fails and the user is logged out mid-task. A promise cache lets the late joiner await the winner instead of racing it.
